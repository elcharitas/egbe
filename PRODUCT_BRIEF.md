# Egbe — product brief

**Proposition:** Egbe helps students bring their own materials into a study
circle, take one manageable step, and return with a question for peers.

## Learner and problem

**Illustrative persona, not a researched user:** Ada is a university student
studying on her phone between other responsibilities, sometimes with unreliable
connectivity. She has notes and classmates but struggles to restart after an
interruption. Interviews and validation of this situation remain outstanding.

The problem occurs when she returns to a half-finished reading and cannot
remember what she understood or what she wanted to ask. Egbe's hypothesis is
that retained context and a clear next action make restarting less demanding.
Improved retention, grades, or learning outcomes have not been demonstrated.

## How Egbe helps

Ada signs in with Google and joins or creates a private or public circle.
Students bring attributed links or permitted text. She chooses a session time,
sets a goal, and works through Read, Explain, and Discuss. Her notes and progress
remain private and return after a confirmed D1 account save.

With configured OpenAI API access, the Study assistant can explain a reviewed
excerpt or suggest practice. It sends the question, excerpt, material title,
and attribution—not private goals or notes automatically. Ada checks the
AI-labelled response against the source, then prepares an editable question for
her circle. Posting remains a separate action.

Unconfirmed edits stay only in the open page, with retry/export controls;
Egbe does not promise persistent offline reload.

## Challenge fit and demonstration

The strongest fit is **sustained engagement**. Flexible sessions, revisitable
steps, and peer check-ins support returning to useful work. Attributed starter
links support discovery; the three-step sequence supports guidance.

A demonstration shows two learners sharing material and a check-in while a
private note remains visible only to its owner. This is supported functionality,
not a measured pilot outcome.

## Non-goals and proposed measures

Egbe is not an LMS, assessment system, moderated network, or content downloader.
It does not retrieve lessons automatically or use ChatGPT subscription credits.

For a small consented pilot, observe completed study/check-in journeys, returns
to saved work, understanding of private versus shared content, and responses
to failed-save warnings. These are proposed measures, not results; interviews
should precede educational-impact claims.
